# Outline: "Founded from Earth" — the Home-System Programme (candidate eighteenth paper)

*Planning document, 2026-09-17. Not a paper. Drafted for the author's reaction before any text moves out of the roadmap paper.*

## Thesis

The Earth era of the lineage is a programme, not a launch. Everything the roadmap paper's ladder needs in quantity, fuel, a launch platform, a census of the solar system, and the first probes outward, can begin now, on Earth and the Moon, in parallel with the ladder and ahead of settlement zero. The programme has four tracks that run at once: the readiness ladder (kept in the roadmap paper), a supply track, a launch platform, and solar-system scouting. Its product is settlement zero provisioned, a lunar platform that can put riders on passing interstellar objects and recurring comets, and a first handful of probes already outward before any off-Earth fuel exists. The paper's one hard question is whether those first probes may carry seeds, since under the no-recall decision an active rider beyond the heliopause is the lineage's first irreversible act.

## Sections

1. **The programme the ladder needs.** The ladder is demonstrators under a no-resupply rule; a programme is everything else. Four parallel tracks, their products, and the one place they meet: the declared inventory.
2. **What Earth has.** The resources the programme starts with, stated as numbers: ~1,250 t of highly enriched uranium in existence (International Panel on Fissile Materials 2022) against ~1 t for the first generation; enrichment and breeding capacity; launch cadence to lunar and belt distances; robotic mining and ISRU at their present readiness (from the roadmap's S0 inventory); the Moon at 2.4 km/s with no atmosphere, and the Moon as a fuel source in its own right: thorium and uranium in the Procellarum KREEP Terrane, water ice in the polar cold traps for propellant and chemistry, and helium-3 in the regolith, which the series notes and does not use because the lineage does not run on fusion. The point: no physical limit binds the Earth era; the limits are governance and time.
3. **The supply track.** Moved from the roadmap and expanded. The Moon first (Procellarum KREEP Terrane; a lunar mine and breeder; U-233 for vigil batteries), the belt (Ceres, carbonaceous and metal bodies, depots cached before S4), Mars and the Trojans, the outer moons as depots and rehearsal sites. The three rules. Fuel: the ppb arithmetic and the breeder conclusion. Settlement zero's reactor fuel as a separate line. An assay campaign: no body beyond Earth has been assayed for uranium in situ.
4. **The launch platform.** The new material. A lunar mass driver for tens of km/s is 50 to 500 km at 100 to 1,000 g, against the roadmap's 1,000 to 10,000 km interstellar driver; energy per launch of an 18-tonne rider at 30 km/s is ~8 × 10^12 J, trivial. Compare an Earth launch (11 km/s well, atmosphere, heavy lift) with the lunar driver; the Moon wins for anything that must leave fast and often. The platform's three jobs: prospectors to the belt and outward, riders to interstellar objects, and rendezvous with recurring comets. Precedents: Rosetta's rendezvous with 67P; Comet Interceptor's wait at L2; the Hoover et al. rendezvous accessibility figures. "Further out of the well, the better": the ordering Moon, then belt, then outer depots, and why settlement zero is still the belt.
5. **Solar-system scouting.** The scouts' main function is detailing our own solar system, body by body, as opposed to the sky survey work of Hubble- and Webb-class observatories. The prospector fleet launched from the platform rather than built at S3: the census the replication model needs, run early and from Earth; radiation and thermal envelopes for each region; the outer-system rehearsal (1 to 4% sunlight on fission; entering a system from its cold edge). What scouting measures that the supply track needs (assay) and that the ladder needs (feedstock fidelity for S1 simulant).
6. **The interstellar-object rider.** Moved from the roadmap: what the body offers, the active window as an Oort-cloud mission, the five, vigil and succession, reach, and fuel. New: placement from the lunar platform rather than from settlement zero; what changes when the rider precedes the settlement (its beacon goes to Earth or the Moon; its ledger's founding key is Earth's).
7. **Recurring comets as platforms.** The distinguishing case: a Jupiter-family or Halley-type comet comes back. A rider on a periodic comet can be serviced and upgraded at each perihelion, which no interstellar rider can be. It is the rehearsal for riders, the smallest moving resource base, and a volatile-rich body that returns to the inner system on a schedule. Rendezvous delta-v of a few km/s (Rosetta class). Because it is serviced, it can carry heavier and more complex instruments than an interstellar rider, and its job, like the scouts', is detailing our own solar system rather than the sky: an assay suite that characterises the comet's own volatiles and every body it passes as a fuel source, feeding the supply track's census; imaging of planets, moons, and small bodies from geometries and, at times, distances no Earth-orbit telescope has, including close passes on an orbit that runs from inside 1 AU to Jupiter's distance; and in-situ sampling of the interplanetary environment along that orbit. Deep-sky astrometry is not its purpose, and the deep-time navigation candidate paper should look to purpose-built observatories for that; the rider's long baseline is at most an opportunistic by-product. What it cannot do: leave the solar system.
8. **The first probes outward.** The hard section. Under the no-recall decision an active rider placed before settlement zero is the lineage's first irreversible act, S5 in the ethics paper's sense, taken decades early, under the least tested core the programme will ever have, by Earth's governance. Three options, argued: (a) wait, and let the driver's first launch be S5; (b) place riders early with the S3-era core, accepting the irreversibility for the hedge it buys; (c) place early riders as observatories only, with no seeds and no manufacturing, so that nothing capable of reproduction leaves the heliopause until the S5 standing is exercised, and add the four seeds to later riders. Option (c) reads the ethics paper's threshold as attaching to the capacity to reproduce rather than to any vehicle, which is a refinement the ethics paper would have to accept. **Author's decision (2026-09-17): a few early seeds are acceptable as test platforms for working out intractable issues, confined to the early phase.** The paper should state that as its position and reconcile it with no recall in two steps. First, the early riders are the only testbed the programme has for the things the roadmap paper says the solar system cannot validate: dormancy, cold restart, ageing without maintenance, autonomy with no one listening; a rider leaving at 26 km/s tests the first decades and centuries of all four in real time, and that is what makes them worth sending early. Second, what makes an early rider a test platform rather than the lineage's first act is what it carries: an early rider tests the resident and, if seeds are aboard, the seeds' dormancy and repair, but a seed capable of reproduction does not leave the heliopause until the S5 standing has been exercised, so early riders are observatories and dormancy testbeds with inert seed hardware, and the reproduction-capable seeds ride later. This reads the ethics paper's threshold as attaching to the capacity to reproduce rather than to any vehicle, a refinement that paper would have to accept, and it confines the test platforms to the early phase by construction: once settlement zero exists, riders are the settlement's to place.
9. **Governance of the Earth era.** Who holds the founding key before settlement zero; ledgered fissile accounting from mine to seed; the dual-use chain (a lunar breeder produces weapons-usable material by the hundred kilograms); the platform as a weapon (a 30 km/s driver on the Moon); the standing question exercised early if option (b) is chosen. Ties to the ethics paper's Section 14 and the governance paper's home-system rider.
10. **Ordering and parallelism.** What starts now (S0 inventory, S1 plant, lunar assay, the supply-track mine), what waits on what, and the decades each takes. The programme runs the tracks in parallel and the ladder in sequence; its critical path is the lunar driver and the breeder, not the ladder.
11. **Failure modes.** Capture or proliferation of the fuel chain; the platform repurposed; scouting that never becomes a census; a depot that corrupts the closure measurement; an early rider carrying a core later revised (the speciation paper's problem from day one); the programme outliving the political window the subsystem budget paper measures in decades.
12. **Relationship to other papers.** The roadmap paper (this is its supply side and its first act); the bootstrapping paper (fissile closure as a breeder problem); the subsystem budget paper (the home programme's window, now relocated to a platform that outlives it); the ethics paper (standing exercised early; the reproduction-capacity reading of the threshold); the governance paper (the builders' rung); the security paper (root capture, now with the platform as a second target); the Fermi paper (the rider as messenger probe); the consistency review's moving-resource-base candidate (the comet rider is its first instance).
13. **Conclusion.**
