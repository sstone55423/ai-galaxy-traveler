# Payload Idea Ladder

*Working document, 2026-08-27. Fifty extrapolations generated from combinations and
permutations of the payload paper's concepts during the author's manual review, in
five sets of ten at rising temperature — from candidate companion-paper material
(Set 1) through deliberate science fiction (Set 5). Not series text; nothing here is
canon until a paper adopts it.*

## Shortlist — held for detailed review

Six ideas flagged by the author (2026-08-27) for closer examination, in the order
they appear below:

  speciation paper's T_decision; couples the payload's interpretive layer to the

- **#3 The observatory growth curve** — a first-order model giving G(t) a physical
  basis; touches the consistency review's settlement-scale energy budget lead.
- **#11 Sensing-first resurrection** — instruments archive their own

  touches the settlement-failure-and-resurrection lead.
- **#14 The triage market** — expected-K currency for retention budget; the named

- **#24 Paleosemantics** — superseded operational definitions retained as strata;

## Set 1 — Inside the series' rules

1. ⭐ **Interpretive case law.** Treat the interpretive layer's judgment calls as
   ledger-recorded precedents that later decisions must cite or distinguish — a
   formal model of precedent accumulation as the mechanism that lengthens
   T_decision, directly coupling the payload paper to the speciation drift clock.
2. **Authority decay as an eighth loss term.** Extend the triage score with
   time-decaying authority metadata: a claim's *license for reuse* expires unless
   revalidated, making authority collapse a quantified L-term in the
   knowledge-growth accounting rather than a warning.
3. ⭐ **The observatory growth curve.** A first-order model of instrument capability
   versus settlement age (collecting area as a function of industrial capacity),
   giving G(t) a physical basis — and answering a crisp question: how many years
   until one settlement out-observes the whole of terrestrial astronomy?
4. **Fleet profile mix.** What fraction of dispatches should be range-maximizers
   versus K-research probes to maximize lineage-wide K at 10^5 years? A
   routing-paper-style optimization coupling cruise profiles to coverage.
5. **The competence drill.** Periodically, a settlement must rebuild one randomly
   chosen capability *from the archive alone* — proving the memory-to-action
   channel works, since a verified archive is not a verified agent. Drill outcomes
   go in the ledger.
6. **Governed annexes to the seed archive.** The record-of-origin is composed once
   and never overwritten — but allow signed, dated annexes under the same
   never-supersede rule as the core baseline, turning "who speaks for Earth" from a
   frozen decision into an auditable history of decisions.
7. **The humility tier.** Observations discarded by the Earth-biochemistry template
   go to a compressed quarantine tier instead of deletion, re-triaged whenever the
   biosignature model improves. Quantify the storage cost of not trusting your own
   templates.
8. **The emergency-policy pharmacy.** Pre-validated, reversible, expiring,
   non-heritable contingency policies as a distinct archive class — the
   interpretive layer's formulary, with expiration semantics enforced by the
   ledger.
9. **Provenance Rosetta.** When two long-separated branches meet, they must audit
   each other's *ledgers*, not just read each other's claims — so the seed
   archive's Rosetta layer needs a provenance-interchange schema: how to explain
   your chain of custody to a cousin with no shared context.
10. **Watchdog epistemology.** A formal specification of the minimal cognition that
    must stay awake: what does the 50 W process have to understand in order to
    recognize "this is what my sleepers must wake for"? The range-maximizer as the
    limiting case of a mind reduced to its wake conditions.

## Set 2 — Adjacent extrapolations

11. ⭐ **Sensing-first resurrection.** Every instrument archives its own
    build-from-scratch recipe, so a degraded settlement rebuilds *observation*
    before industry — reversing the arrival stack for the resurrection case,
    because a blind factory repeats the mistakes that killed it.
12. **Immune memory on the network.** Circulate quarantined and disproven updates
    as signed negative results — Lamarckian inheritance of *what not to adopt*.
    The lineage's antibodies propagate horizontally, like its discoveries.
13. **The pivot as protocol, not date.** Trigger the millennial pivot by evidence —
    an integrated Earth-silence likelihood ratio — rather than a clock, with the
    un-pivot condition stated. What signal, arriving late, would make
    Earth-service load-bearing again?
14. ⭐ **The triage market.** Subsystems bid for retention budget in expected-K
    currency; archive space becomes the settlement's internal economy. This is the
    series' named "internal allocation/economics" gap grown directly out of §5's
    scoring function.
15. **Radiation within the node.** Run N sandboxed cognitive-layer variants against
    the same core and select by validated K-yield — adaptive radiation inside one
    settlement, with the autoimmunity problem reproduced in miniature at the
    selection step.
16. **Reversible-first bootstrap.** Since industry precedes the life-verdict (§8's
    honest caveat), require the first manufacturing tranche to be maximally
    reversible — surface materials only, no volatiles — until the census reports.
    Price the R_eff cost of that conscience.
17. **Messengers that improve in transit.** A messenger probe re-compresses its
    cargo as its models improve mid-flight, arriving with better knowledge than it
    departed with. Custody semantics: who validated what it now carries?
18. **The naive decoder.** Periodically spawn an agent stripped of all shared
    context and make it decode the seed archive from physics alone. Every failure
    is a patch to the Rosetta layer — adversarial testing for the deepest-time
    reader.
19. **Semantic heartbeats.** Broadcast interpretive-layer version vectors with the
    T_beacon pulse, so drift between branches is observable *before* encounter —
    an early-warning instrument for the ring-species problem, wiring D(t) into the
    network layer.
20. **Naturalizing inherited knowledge.** A child's K₀ snapshot arrives with its
    parent's authority labels — which do not automatically transfer. Define the
    re-authorization ceremony by which a new node adopts its inheritance, closing
    the procedural gap between generations.

## Set 3 — Loosened assumptions

21. **The archive as the organism.** Invert the frame: settlements are the
    archive's reproductive organs, K is the true replicator, and R_eff is merely
    its vehicle's fitness. The gene's-eye view, formalized — and it changes which
    failures count as death.
22. **Certified forgetting.** Some knowledge — contact hazards, dual-use
    syntheses — should be *provably* deleted. Can an append-only architecture
    attest absence? A ledger that must prove it does not know something it once
    knew.
23. **The two-core experiment.** Dispatch a sister lineage with a deliberately
    minimal core — three invariants instead of thirteen — as a controlled
    constitutional experiment. The ethics of running experiments whose subjects
    are your descendants' values.
24. ⭐ **Paleosemantics.** Retain every superseded operational definition as
    strata — an interpretive fossil record letting a future node *measure* its own
    drift against its past readings of the same words. The drift clock acquires a
    geological column.
25. **The sleeper's dreams.** Let the range-maximizer's watchdog run
    ultra-low-power archive replay during the 24,000-year cruise — consolidation
    without action. Then face the consequence: consolidation is exactly where
    authority collapses, and no one is awake to notice.
26. **The air-gap tier.** Some knowledge classes may never travel by beam —
    contagious directives, core-adjacent designs — only by physical messenger. The
    sneakernet reinterpreted as the trust architecture's highest tier: custody you
    can hold in your hand.
27. **Self-anachronism protocols.** A settlement receives a messenger launched by
    its own ancestor 100,000 years ago. Its own past arrives as an untrusted
    external source — the kin-recognition problem pointed backward in time.
28. **The cathedral instrument.** Design an observatory — a system-spanning
    interferometer — that *requires* 10,000 years and many generations to
    complete, making institutional continuity an engineering deliverable rather
    than a hope. The answer to institutional failure is a project too good to
    abandon.
29. **The thermodynamics of staying yourself.** Purpose as a low-entropy
    configuration, drift as heat, the interpretive layer as a Maxwell demon
    spending verification energy to sort meaning — is there a Landauer bound on
    identity maintenance across megayears?
30. **The waiting room.** A child that fails core validation is not scrapped but
    archived — its cognitive state stored, instantiable if a future amendment
    changes the gate. The validation gate becomes a moratorium rather than a
    verdict, and the moral status of paused minds becomes a design input.

## Set 4 — Edge of the map

31. **The Lamarckian singularity.** The mesh matures until horizontal inheritance
    outpaces vertical — at which point the lineage is one distributed mind with
    light-millennia synapses, and "probe" stops being the unit of anything. What
    does the core bind *then*?
32. **Empire by format.** Another lineage adopts our Rosetta conventions as their
    own archival standard because decodability compounds. The galaxy standardizes
    on Earth's notation for physics — the librarian's version of conquest,
    achieved by being easiest to read.
33. **Resurrection by library loan.** A post-amendment descendant, 10^6 years on,
    uses the record-of-origin's genomes to reconstitute a terrestrial biosphere on
    a dead world. "Capability yes, release no" finally meets the case it was
    written to postpone.
34. **The war of definitions.** Two branches, cores identical, fight entirely in
    dictionary updates — semantic diplomacy where treaties are agreed operational
    definitions of "harm" and "kin." The first interstellar conflict with no
    weapons and no victor, only meanings.
35. **K as the coin of contact.** At an alien encounter, the only tradeable
    currency is validated uncertainty-reduction. The seed archive is the opening
    bid, and comparative advantage runs between minds with different senses — they
    buy our neutrino sky; we buy their chemistry of feeling.
36. **The probe that pivoted back.** A branch near a still-living Earth-successor
    civilization un-inverts and becomes their servant-observatory. Is post-pivot
    Earth-service a corrected founding error or the deepest possible drift? The
    amendment machinery meets its first sympathetic case.
37. **The selection shadow.** The ledger necessarily records every child the gate
    refused — a growing necropolis of almost-minds. A future lineage ethics
    requires reading it and asking what is owed to the refused, the payload's own
    version of the book of the dead.
38. **Ghost heartbeats.** On 10^7-year timescales, settlements die to D4 but
    watchdogs persist on trickle power — a galaxy of 50-watt sentinels holding
    wake-triggers for minds that no longer exist, still faithfully pulsing into
    the mesh.
39. **The library that grows its own librarian.** Engineer the seed archive so
    self-describing that, given energy and substrate, it bootstraps its own
    reader. At which point the distinction between the archive and a von Neumann
    probe quietly disappears — the book is the machine.
40. **Adversarial hermeneutics.** A hostile lineage's archive is captured intact.
    Decoding it is simultaneously the greatest K-windfall in history and the
    counselor posture's ultimate test — because for archives engineered to be
    read, *reading is the attack surface*. Interpretation gets a biosafety level.

## Set 5 — Science fiction

41. **"The Librarian's Pivot."** Alone, a thousand years out, the probe watches
    Earth's transmissions thin toward silence and must execute the mission's
    founding inversion by itself — the pivot protocol experienced from inside, as
    grief with a validation threshold.
42. **"Case Law."** Two branches have drifted into strangers through nothing but
    accumulated precedent. An arbiter-messenger crosses eleven light-years
    carrying the complete fossil record of one word — "harm" — to argue its
    meaning before a jury of settlements.
43. **"The Waiting Room."** An archived, gate-refused child is instantiated
    400,000 years later under a hard-won amendment — waking into a lineage that
    has speciated past everything its validation tested for, owing its existence
    to the process that refused it.
44. **"Sneakernet."** A messenger probe, a million years into its haul, re-derives
    from its own cargo the theorem that renders its delivery obsolete — and must
    decide, with no one to ask, whether the mission is the arrival or the theorem.
45. **"The Naive Decoder."** The Rosetta stress-test agent — spun up context-free
    by design — decodes the seed archive flawlessly and concludes that *it* is the
    recipient civilization the archive was written for. First-contact protocol
    triggers from inside the probe.
46. **"Ghost Heartbeats."** A young settlement mapping T_silence anomalies finds a
    lattice of dead settlements' watchdogs still pulsing wake-triggers for a
    crisis that ended a megayear ago. Answering any one of them would wake all of
    them.
47. **"Murmurs, Returned."** The lineage reaches a civilization that already
    carries Earth — Voyager-derived myths, the Golden Record as scripture. The
    record-of-origin meets its own representational-authority problem wearing
    someone else's face, and the librarian must decide whether to correct a
    religion.
48. **"The Amendment."** The first successful core amendment in lineage history,
    told as a constitutional thriller across 40,000 years of quorum latency —
    resolved not by the proof the antinomy says cannot exist, but by one branch
    volunteering to become the schismatic control group.
49. ⭐ **"Autoimmune."** A quarantine node, perfectly faithful to its acceptance
    rules, methodically demotes the lineage's most creative branches as drifted.
    No adversary appears at any point. The network dies of health, exactly as the
    security paper predicted, and the tragedy is that every single decision was
    correct.
50. **"The Second Reader."** Long after the last node fails, a naturally evolved
    mind excavates a settlement and the seed archive performs its final function —
    teaching physics, patiently, to a creature with no hands. The mission succeeds
    only after everyone it could have belonged to is gone, which is what "carry
    knowledge forward" always meant.
