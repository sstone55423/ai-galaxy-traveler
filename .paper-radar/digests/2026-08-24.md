### Payload paper — Section 5, "From Data to Knowledge"

**Compositional neurosymbolic representations enable efficient active
exploration** — Furlong, P. M., Dumont, N. S.-Y., Antonova, R., Orchard, J., &
Eliasmith, C. (2026), *Nature Communications*
[doi](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-026-75703-4) · verified via OpenAlex
(title, authors, year 2026, venue *Nature Communications* all match)

Bears on: the claim that "a probe that can observe more than it can store,
model, or transmit is defined by what it throws away" — currently framed as a
storage-and-bandwidth constraint. This attaches a measured complexity bound to
a pressure the paper asserts qualitatively, and shows part of it is a
representational choice rather than a hard limit.

Proposed prose:

> The pressure is not only storage. Standard active-exploration methods scale
> with the number of observations accumulated — O(n^3) time and O(n^2) memory
> for state-of-the-art Bayesian optimization — which by construction rules out
> sustained operation on bounded hardware, precisely the probe's situation over
> a megayear archive. Furlong et al. (2026) show that compositional
> high-dimensional vector representations hold both costs at O(d^2) in a fixed
> embedding dimension, constant over the algorithm's lifetime, cutting compute
> time 60–200× without accuracy loss and, on neuromorphic hardware, energy per
> sample 30–188×. The result is narrow — one algorithm class, laboratory tasks
> — but it establishes that part of what forces triage is a choice of
> representation rather than a hard limit, and it is the kind of choice that
> matters when the cruise power budget is 50–150 W rather than kilowatts.

Reference if accepted: Furlong, P. M., Dumont, N. S.-Y., Antonova, R.,
Orchard, J., & Eliasmith, C. (2026). Compositional neurosymbolic
representations enable efficient active exploration. *Nature Communications*.
https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-026-75703-4

Confidence: medium — every number quoted is in the abstract, but the transfer
from Bayesian optimization to the probe's general cognitive layer is inference,
not a result the authors claim. Held back from staging on that basis. It also
has a secondary bearing on the subsystem budget paper's 50–150 W cruise draw if
you decide the point belongs there instead.
