### Payload paper — §3, A Mind That Improves Without Drifting

**Safety and Performance Considerations in Shielded Autonomous Spacecraft
Tasking** — Quevedo Mantovani, L., & Schaub, H. (2026), *Journal of Aerospace
Information Systems* [doi](https://doi.org/10.2514/1.I011774) · verified via
OpenAlex (title, authors, year, venue all match)

Bears on: the claim closing the Si and Qin (2026) paragraph — that the
architecture "buys stability structurally — a small core reproduced exactly,
outside the learner — rather than by constraining the cognitive layer's capacity
to change."

> Spacecraft autonomy already implements a weak version of this structure. In
> shielded deep reinforcement learning for agile Earth-observing satellites, a
> safety shield sits outside the learned policy and blocks actions violating
> system constraints; training that replaces unsafe actions while penalizing
> shield intervention yields both higher cumulative reward and lower shield
> interference at test time (Quevedo Mantovani and Schaub, 2026). The horizon
> there is a scheduling one, not a megayear one, but the design lesson
> transfers: a constraint enforced outside the learner costs least when the
> learner is trained so that it rarely binds — which is the regime the immutable
> core must hold over deep time.

Reference: Quevedo Mantovani, L., & Schaub, H. (2026). Safety and performance
considerations in shielded autonomous spacecraft tasking. *Journal of Aerospace
Information Systems*. https://doi.org/10.2514/1.I011774

Confidence: medium. The retrieved abstract is truncated; the prose above claims
only the action-replacement and intervention-penalty result, which the abstract
states in full. The domain gap (hours-scale satellite scheduling vs. megayear
autonomy) is flagged in the prose rather than papered over. Held back from
staging on that basis — worth a look before the next payload revision.

## Flagged (no insertion proposed)

- **Nonnaturalism and the Ineffability of Moral Normativity** — Schwarz, L.
  (2026), *Journal of Moral Philosophy*
  [doi](https://doi.org/10.1163/17455243-20254429) · verified via OpenAlex
  The only candidate squarely in the ethics paper's own subfield. If moral
  normativity resists informative literal characterization, then exact
  reproduction of a value *formulation* is not exact reproduction of a *value* —
  a metaethical version of the interpretive-drift problem the speciation and
  governed-amendment papers already handle. Bears on value-imposition in the
  immutable core; the connection would be the author's construction, not the
  paper's finding.

- **Global DNA Barcoding of Sigara (Hemiptera: Corixidae) Reveals Cryptic
  Species Formation and Climatic-Niche Divergence** — Liang, Ren, Sun, & Xie
  (2026), *Insects* [doi](https://doi.org/10.3390/insects17080876) · verified
  via Crossref (too recent for OpenAlex)
  Bimodal COI divergence — intraspecific under 1%, interspecific 8–16%, a sparse
  4–8% intermediate band — is empirical precedent for the speciation paper's
  D_critical: divergence in a real lineage is not a smooth continuum but has a
  gap, with a thin genuinely ambiguous zone. The barcode-gap *concept* is what
  the paper needs; the canonical sources would carry it better than one
  freshwater-insect genus. Noted as calibration precedent.

- **Ad Hoc Prevention of Double-spending in Offline Payments** — Aghapour, A. M.,
  Bahrami, E., & Amini, M. (2026), *SN Computer Science*
  [doi](https://doi.org/10.1007/s42979-026-05259-z) · verified via OpenAlex
  Conflicting-state commitment under total loss of contact with any authority,
  resolved by gossip over opportunistic contacts with Bloom-filter digests to
  bound exchange overhead — structurally the lineage-network paper's ledger-audit
  fork reconciliation. Analogy only; the mechanisms are payment-specific and
  tuned to seconds-to-hours contact windows, not millennium-scale partition. The
  compact-digest idea may still inform how a reconciliation bundle summarises
  accumulated ledger state within a bounded bundle size.

- **Communicative Action in the Age of Artificial Intelligence: Theorizing LLMs
  as Communicative and Strategic Agents** — Hila, A. A. (2026), *Philosophy &
  Technology* [doi](https://doi.org/10.1007/s13347-026-01168-4) · verified via
  Crossref (too recent for OpenAlex)
  A gradualist criterion for artificial agency — LLMs as asymmetrical
  communicative agents satisfying behavioral but not intentional conditions.
  Right kind of argument for the ethics paper's moral-status and legal-personhood
  section, but it says nothing about moral standing, obligations to created
  agents, or unsupervised autonomy, which is what that section needs.

- **An AI System for Autonomous Algorithm Evolution in Drug Development** —
  Zhou, Z., Nan, Y., Mou, M., & Qian, Y. (2026), *bioRxiv* preprint
  [doi](https://doi.org/10.64898/2026.08.16.745117) · verified via OpenAlex;
  unreviewed preprint

  claim needs it; relevant only if that literature is ever surveyed.
