### Knowledge-growth paper — Section 5, Cruise-Phase Operational K: AI Self-Improvement

**Closing the Empirical Loop: Autonomous AI Agents Conduct End-to-end Research
With Human Participants** — Wehr, G., Rideaux, R., Fox, A., Lightfoot, D. R.,
Tangen, J. M., Mattingley, J. B., & Ehrhardt, S. E. (2026), *Advanced Science*
https://doi.org/10.1002/advs.76675 · verified via DOI content negotiation
(title, all seven authors, year, venue)

Bears on: the section's existence-proof argument, which currently rests on
Mechanist (Wang et al. 2026) — a system investigating its own internals. This is
the outward case: the full empirical loop closed on the world.

> The loop has also been closed outward, on the world rather than on the system's
> own internals. A domain-agnostic agentic system autonomously designed and
> executed three psychological studies, ran online data collection with 288
> participants, built its analysis pipelines, and produced completed manuscripts,
> with theoretical reasoning and methodological rigor the authors judge
> comparable to experienced researchers (Wehr et al. 2026). The same work names
> the limitation that matters most here: the weaknesses were in conceptual nuance
> and theoretical interpretation, not in execution. Cruise-phase operational K
> should be budgeted on that shape — reliable on the procedural and analytical
> side, discounted wherever a result turns on reframing the question rather than
> answering it.

Confidence: high. Peer-reviewed, and every clause above is stated in the abstract.

### DNA mission-ledger paper — Section 4, Coding, Encapsulation, and Redundancy

**Identifying Damage Pathways Linking Sequence Composition to Storage Failure in
DNA Data Storage via High-Dimensional Mediation Analysis** — Li, J., Wu, H., &
Zhang, H. (2026), arXiv:2609.19822 https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2609.19822
· verified via DOI content negotiation (title, all three authors, arXiv, 2026)

Bears on: the codec-selection paragraph, which currently says no codec is optimal
across substitutions, insertions and deletions and leaves the choice open. This
narrows it — the dominant damage pathway measured is deletions, not substitutions.

> Which error types the code must carry is less open than the substitution-first
> framing suggests. A high-dimensional mediation analysis of an aging experiment
> on electrochemically synthesized DNA finds that every one of the 14 damage
> components significantly linking sequence composition to storage failure is a
> single-base deletion, with the mediated effects concentrated in trinucleotide
> contexts ending in C (Li, Wu & Zhang 2026). Two requirements follow for the
> archive: the code must be indel-correcting rather than substitution-only, and
> the encoder must treat local sequence context, not gross GC fraction alone, as
> a constraint on which strands it is permitted to emit. One aging experiment on
> one synthesis chemistry fixes the direction of that requirement, not its
> magnitude.

Confidence: high on the finding, which is fully stated in the abstract. It is an
unrefereed arXiv preprint, and the prose is scoped to the one chemistry tested.

### Governed-amendment paper — Section 2, The Dilemma, Stated Precisely

**Annual Review of Constitution-Building: 2025** — Abebe, A. K., Hickey, S. P.,
Hudson, A., Montero Ibarra, E., Oancea, A., & Zulueta-Fülscher, K. (2026),
International IDEA https://doi.org/10.31752/18859 · verified via DOI content
negotiation (title, all six authors, publisher, 2026) · **carried over** from
2026-09-14, where it was flagged rather than proposed

Bears on: both horns of the dilemma — that immutability is brittle and that
amendability relocates the drift hazard into the amendment procedure. The section
argues this analytically; this is the empirical record of both failures in one year.

> The terrestrial record shows both horns drawing blood in the same year.
> International IDEA's review of constitution-building in 2025 reports barriers to
> constitutional change rigid enough to block reform in Fiji and The Gambia, and,
> in the same survey, constitutional reform used to entrench personalized rule in
> Chad, Djibouti, El Salvador, Guinea, and Nicaragua (Abebe et al. 2026). The
> review's remedy is instructive for what it assumes: inclusive processes, broad
> political agreement, and effective safeguards against the concentration of power
> — contemporaneous political resources, not better drafting. A lineage separated
> by light-centuries has the drafting and none of the rest.

Confidence: high. The country lists and the remedy are stated verbatim in the
abstract; nothing is extrapolated.

## Proposed and not staged (author's decision)

Each is verified and each came back with a specific insertion, but none clears the

- **Temporal connectivity of random geometric graphs** — Brandenberger, A.,
  Donderwinkel, S., Kerriou, C., Lugosi, G., & Mitchell, R. (2026), *Journal of
  Applied Probability* https://doi.org/10.1017/jpr.2026.10129 · verified.
  **The most interesting item in the sweep**, and the only one that challenges a
  number rather than decorating it: in temporal random geometric graphs, where
  only time-ordered paths propagate, all-pairs temporal connectivity sets in at a
  markedly higher edge density than simple connectivity, and the gap is specific
  to the geometric case. A settlement front is monotone by construction, so the
  routing paper's 93%-at-20-ly is an upper bound on time-ordered coverage rather
  than a prediction of it. Not staged because their edge time-stamps are uniformly
  random while dispatch times are generated by the front itself — it bounds the
  direction of the error, not its size, and that argument is the author's to make.
- **Artificial Id: Drive and Persistent Alignment in Agentic AI** — Shkolnikov,
  Y. P. (2026), arXiv:2609.11911 https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2609.11911 ·
  verified · **carried over**; proposed for the payload paper on 09-14 and not
  taken. Today's screen rates it higher (persistence alone producing an effective
  drive, and carrying corrupted state forward, as the argument for a structural
  core rather than a held commitment). Unrefereed single-author preprint; it is
  the same item, not a second opinion.
- **Speciation, extinction and explosions induced by sudden environmental
  changes** — Landucci, L. G., & de Aguiar, M. A. M. (2026), arXiv:2609.13349 ·
  identifier absent from the harvested record and **resolved here** via the arXiv
  API (title, both authors, submitted 2026-09-11). A genuine
  assortativity-threshold speciation model, so the closest formal analogue to
  D_critical: tightening the threshold triggers a speciation burst that settles at
  a higher steady species count, and a sudden carrying-capacity rise can instead
  collapse the system onto one dominant species depending on how many loci control
  compatibility. The second half qualifies the adaptive-radiation argument, which
  is why it deserves the author's own reading before insertion.
- **Cryothermal Measurements of Variable-Emittance Coatings with Lower Phase
  Transition Temperatures for Space Thermal Control** — Stoops, C., Rajan, V. K.,
  & Wang, L. (2026), arXiv:2609.14293 · identifier absent from the record and
  **resolved here** via the arXiv API. W-doped VO2 measured against a cold-space
  background: emittance swing 0.4 to 0.6, up to 3.5x more radiative dissipation,
  transition temperature about 25 C lower at 1 at.% W. Bounds the obvious
  objection to the subsystem paper's two-mode radiator result — a turndown of that
  order buys margin within cruise mode, not a second mode, since A_mfg / A_cruise
  is ~10^2.
- **The Abiotic Background: Challenges and Opportunities in the Search for
  Evidence of Life Beyond Earth** — Davila, A., Hoehler, T., Shkolyar, S., et al.
  (2026), *Astrobiology* https://doi.org/10.1177/15311074261484864 · verified.
  A large abiotic background can mimic, obscure or alter biosignatures in both
  directions under hard payload constraints — the evidence base for where the
  governance paper sets its E-rung detection threshold. Peer-reviewed; not staged
  only because the insertion needs the actual rung-assignment heading, which the
  screen could not see.
- **Ex-Situ and In-Situ Hybrid Processing Opportunities in Metal Additive
  Manufacturing** — Ramasamy, V., Qu, M., & Lewandowski, J. J. (2026),
  *Materials* https://doi.org/10.3390/ma19183927 · verified. As-built AM defects
  routinely require secondary steps — heat treatment, HIP, deformation processing,
  machining — and which step matters is alloy-dependent. Each is another machine
  in the seed, which is the bootstrapping paper's 54% plant fraction argued from
  process evidence rather than asserted. Terrestrial review, no closure numbers.
- **Cooperative Evolutionary Pressure and Diminishing Returns Might Explain the
  Fermi Paradox** — Vallstrom, D. (2026), OSF Preprints
  https://doi.org/10.31219/osf.io/bq438_v7 · verified. Two arguments against the
  R_eff term: no replication is exponential indefinitely, and diminishing returns
  may leave a mature lineage with no incentive to take the whole galaxy even at
  R_eff > 1. The second is a genuine off-ramp the Fermi paper does not have.
  Downgraded from the screen's high confidence: self-published, unrefereed, at v7.
- **A Dark Forest First Attack Is Rational Only If the Attacker Accepts It as Its
  Last** — Dam, H. (2026), arXiv:2609.12003
  https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2609.12003 · verified. Removes deliberate
  concealment as a rival account of galactic silence, which the Fermi paper's D
  term would otherwise have to argue against. Game theory of strike-versus-silence
  rather than detectability limits, so it fits only if the paper engages the Dark
  Forest family at all.
