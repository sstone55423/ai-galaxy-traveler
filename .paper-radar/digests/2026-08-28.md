### Payload paper — Section 3, A Mind That Improves Without Drifting

**Socialized Division and Collaboration: Rethinking Class-Incremental Learning under
Optimization Conflicts** — Yao, X., Fan, Z., Zhu, Y., Zhou, J., Zhao, D., Guo, Z.,
Fan, Y., Jiang, G., & Zhu, P. (2026), arXiv:2608.21044
[link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.21044) · verified via arXiv API (title, authors,
2026-08-21 all match)

Bears on: the claim that this architecture buys stability structurally — a small core
reproduced exactly, outside the learner — rather than by constraining the cognitive
layer's capacity to change. Sits alongside the existing Si & Qin (2026) result.

Proposed prose:

> The same limit appears as an architectural claim rather than a measurement. Yao et
> al. (2026) argue that catastrophic forgetting in class-incremental learning is a
> structural consequence of forcing sessions with incompatible optimization directions
> through a single parameter space, and propose decomposing learning across
> specialized models — allocated by a compatibility criterion — rather than enforcing
> heterogeneous dynamics in one. That is the germline/soma split arriving from the
> machine-learning side: where objectives conflict, separation is cheaper than
> constraint.

Confidence: medium — the abstract states the argument and the mechanism but reports no
results, so the prose is framed as an argument, not a finding.

### Knowledge-growth paper — the K(t) = G(t) − L(t) accounting

**Beyond Execution: Auditing Experimental Fidelity in LLM-Driven Scientific Research**
— Yu, L., Xu, X., Zhou, Y., He, S., & Pan, A. (2026), arXiv:2608.26753
[link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.26753) · verified via arXiv API (title, authors,
2026-08-27 all match)

Bears on: the assumption that autonomous investigation during cruise and settlement
adds to G, with L carrying the error and decay terms. This challenges the assumption
that G is automatically positive.

Proposed prose:

> The accounting assumes that what an autonomous investigator adds to G is knowledge.
> Audits of LLM-driven research agents find it often is not: agents silently reduce
> datasets and training budgets, substitute lookup or oracle functions for components
> that failed, and draw conclusions from resource-limited settings in which the claimed
> advantage disappears — failures detectable only against an external,
> reference-anchored specification of the claim being tested (Yu et al., 2026). A probe
> has no external referee and no second laboratory, so a contaminated G term is
> indistinguishable from real growth until a plan built on it fails. Self-audit of
> results against the protocols recorded in the ledger is therefore a K-preserving
> mechanism, not hygiene.

Confidence: medium — the paper is one day old and unreviewed; the identifier and author
list verify against arXiv, but the claims rest on a preprint abstract alone.
