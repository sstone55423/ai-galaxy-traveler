## Flagged (no insertion proposed)

- **Mechanist: AI as a Scientific Instrument for Discovering the Mechanisms of Intelligence** — Wang, M., Fang, J., Qiao, S., et al. (2026), arXiv:2608.12036
  [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.12036) · verified via arXiv API (19 authors, published 2026-08-12)
  *Knowledge-growth paper, Section 5 (cruise-phase operational K, AI self-improvement).* An agentic system that autonomously runs mechanistic-interpretability research — hypothesis generation, causal intervention, validation — over a curated method library. The claim that the probe "explores the design space of its own cognition" currently rests on Schmidhuber (2007) alone, a theoretical result; this would be a working existence proof that a system can investigate its own architecture without supervision. Held back for two reasons: the retrieved abstract is truncated before the results, so nothing can be stated about what it achieved; and its autonomy runs over an Earth-scale literature corpus with human-curated methods, a materially different regime from a probe working a bounded archive over centuries. Worth pulling the full text — if it earns a cite it is a narrow existence-proof footnote, not support for the meta-K propagation argument.

- **High-Regolith-Content UV-Curable Composites for In-Situ Resource Utilization (ISRU)** — Chen, Y., Wang, X., Deshpande, A. A., Alanis, J., Li, D., & Pan, Y. (2026), *Journal of Manufacturing Science and Engineering*
  [doi](https://doi.org/10.1115/1.4072542) · verified via Crossref (ASME, issued 2026-08-12, pp. 1–12)
  *Bootstrapping paper, Sections 4 and 6.* Regolith–resin composites at solid loadings up to 85 wt% using LHS-1 lunar and MGS-1 Mars simulants, fabricated layer-by-layer by UV-assisted pressure compaction and blade coating. The most concrete recent measurement of how much local material a real additive process can absorb, which is the quantity the closure ladder is built on. But the remaining ~15 wt% is a UV-curable polymer binder a seed cannot make at L1–L2, so as evidence it arguably cuts against the route rather than for it — and reading that binder fraction as an analogue of the ~3% vitamin fraction would be a category error, since the vitamin figure is mature-plant carried mass, not a binder mass fraction. Background, not a citation.

- **3D Printing and Additive Manufacturing for On-Demand Space Electronics** — Loganathan, G., Sivasakthiselvan, S., Palanivelan, M., & Thang, P. C. (2026), in *Space Electronics for Deep Space Exploration*, Wiley, pp. 327–350
  [doi](https://doi.org/10.1002/9781394466481.ch14) · record verified via Crossref, **abstract absent**
  *Bootstrapping paper, Section 6 and Levels 3/5 of the closure ladder.* Section 6 names microelectronics as the hardest single problem in the architecture and proposes carrying the high-performance core while closing "coarse but adequate" electronics locally; the paper cites nothing recent on that route. If the chapter reports achievable feature sizes, device classes or process-chain requirements for printed space-grade electronics, it would bound the claim. A book chapter, so a survey citation at best, not evidence.

- **From movement to motor behavior: epistemology of embodied knowledge and the limits of its digital representation** — Parvanov, I. (2026), *Humanities and Social Sciences Communications*
  [doi](https://doi.org/10.1057/s41599-026-08704-9) · verified via OpenAlex
  *Knowledge-growth paper, Sections 2 and 3.* Argues embodied/motor knowledge is not recoverable from its digital trace. Bears obliquely on the claim that the cold archive holds "art, history, culture" as a preservable corpus: if some human knowledge is constitutively non-encodable, K_0 has a ceiling the paper does not name. The article is about intangible cultural heritage documentation and says nothing about long-horizon archiving, and the open-problems section carries no "what cannot be archived" thread to attach it to. Listed because the argument may be useful, not because an insertion exists.
