### Governed-amendment paper — Section 8, "Constitutional amendment by analogy"

**Cardano's Voltaire Governance: Complete Specification and Research Program** — Talmon, N., & Elem, O. (2026), arXiv:2607.11601 · [link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.11601) · verified via web search (exact title, authors, and 2026-07-13 date confirmed)

Bears on: the paragraph arguing the constitutional-amendment analogy "imports an enforcement context the probe lacks: a continuous polity … courts and institutions that make the rules bite." Voltaire is a real, non-human instance of self-amending governance that removes the sovereign/court context — so it fits the probe's setting more closely than a human polity — yet still secures only process, not correctness. It reinforces the section's punchline and echoes the Byzantine-agreement paragraph's "a quorum of drifted lineages can ratify a corrupt amendment with perfect protocol fidelity."

Proposed prose (append to the "Constitutional amendment by analogy" paragraph, §8):

> The analogy has a sharper, non-human instance: decentralized protocols now implement self-amending governance on-chain. Cardano's Voltaire framework specifies a constitutional layer, typed governance actions, and voting rules, and reduces them to a minimal executable governance kernel — a state-transition system on which safety and liveness can be rigorously analyzed (Talmon & Elem 2026). This is legitimate rule change without a central authority, and it fits the probe's setting better than a human polity on the axis that matters — no sovereign, no court — yet it fails in the same place: safety and liveness certify that a well-formed proposal was ratified without deadlock or fork, not that the amendment is correct, and a drifted quorum can ratify a corrupt change with every property intact.

Reference to add (alphabetical, in §References): Talmon, N., & Elem, O. (2026). Cardano's Voltaire Governance: Complete Specification and Research Program. *arXiv*:2607.11601.

Confidence: medium. Held back from staging because it enriches the analogy rather than supplying a load-bearing result. The wording tracks the abstract — a formal governance kernel "enabling rigorous safety and liveness analysis" — and claims nothing beyond it.
