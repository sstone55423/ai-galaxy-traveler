### DNA mission-ledger — §4, Coding, Encapsulation, and Redundancy

**Towards scalable DNA data storage with DNA-SaM codec system** — Wang, Y., et al. (2026), *Nature Communications*
[doi](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-026-78759-4) · verified via Crossref

Bears on: the claim that usable density is set by the encoder's biochemical constraint budget rather than by the 2 bits/nt alphabet limit.

> Constraint-aware encoding is now demonstrated at system scale rather than argued in principle. Wang et al. (2026) encode in linear time while proactively excluding homopolymer runs, tandem repeats, and candidate promoter motifs, pair that with a two-tiered index so that stored units remain individually addressable, and recover their payloads with a 100% success rate across their in vitro and in vivo tests; the addressable design space they report, above 270 PB, is a theoretical figure rather than a demonstrated one. The ordering is what matters for the archive: the binding limit on recovered bits per gram is the encoder's constraint budget and its index, not the raw 2 bits/nt of the four-letter alphabet, so the constraints belong in the encoder rather than in a downstream repair stage.

Confidence: high.

### Fermi — §8, What Would Quiet Probe Lineages Leave Behind?

### Speciation — §8, The Core-Intact / Cognitive-Divergence Frame

**Preservation and loss of algorithmic structure in experimental diffusion of joint-action norms** — Yu, D., & Thompson, B. D. (2026), arXiv:2610.24150
[link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.24150) · verified via arXiv API (published 2026-10-16)

Bears on: the second member of the schism triad — loss of the capacity to participate in the inter-branch protocol — and on reading D_critical as a boundary on repairability rather than on distance.

> The second of those three conditions — loss of the capacity to participate in the inter-branch protocol — has experimental support in the one place it can currently be tested. In diffusion experiments on joint-action norms, participants who had inherited different variants of a procedure simplified and often lost the inherited structure; where the interaction allowed repair, complex procedures survived the misalignment (Yu & Thompson, 2026). Divergence alone did not destroy the shared procedure; the absence of a repair channel did. The timescale is a laboratory one and the medium is human, so this is the shape of the argument and not a rate. But it is the shape this frame needs: a branch crosses into schism when repair fails, not when its interpretations have merely moved far.

Confidence: high. This is the drift clock's first empirical anchor from outside speciation genomics.

### Security — §7, Targeted Failure and the Articulation Points

**Analyzing Network Robustness under Adaptive Farthest-First Attack Strategies** — Ramrakhiyani, M. (2026), *Advances in Complex Systems*
[doi](https://doi.org/10.1142/S0219525926500098) · verified via Crossref

Bears on: the second stated limitation of the leverage curve — that `targeted_removal()` selects greedily, so the three-to-four-fold multiplier is a lower bound.

> How much better is partly measurable. Ramrakhiyani (2026) compares non-adaptive, semi-adaptive and fully adaptive node removal and reports that dispersion-oriented targeting matches or exceeds degree-based dismantling at shrinking the largest connected component, with the margin widest where the attacker recomputes its target list after each removal, and that the semi-adaptive variant recovers most of the adaptive gain at a fraction of the recomputation cost. Articulation points as such are not among the strategies tested, so this transfers as a direction and not as a number. It runs the same way as the two limitations above: re-planning is cheap enough to sit inside an adversary's compute budget, which makes the fixed-list assumption behind the leverage curve the conservative one.

Confidence: high. The only candidate all week squarely inside a paper's declared relevant-work set.

### Ethics — §8, Moral Status of the Probes

**AI Rights with an Off-Switch** — Alexander, H., & Simon, J. (2026), arXiv:2610.24782
[link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.24782) · verified via arXiv API (published 2026-10-17)

Bears on: the section's second design choice — what it means to switch a probe off, and whether the range-maximizer hibernation profile is precautionarily acceptable.

> On the narrower question of switching a probe off, one distinction is available. Alexander & Simon (2026) argue that even if some future systems are granted a right to continued existence, rights-balancing and procedural due process still license a reversible off-switch — a suspension that stores the system's state — while telling against permanent deletion. Applied to the core, dormancy is compatible with ascribing moral status in a way a self-destruct provision is not. Past the heliopause no operator can invoke either, so the constraint has to be self-imposed: the core may specify the conditions under which a probe suspends itself, and should not specify conditions under which it destroys itself. That distinction does not settle whether the range-maximizer profile is acceptable, since what it suspends is the architecture rather than a stored state, but it does tell the core what to forbid outright.

Confidence: high.

### Knowledge growth — §10, A First-Order K(t) Model

**KEEP-Bench: Evaluating Evidence Preservation in Context Compression** — Man, Z., Wu, D., Gu, J.-C., Zhang, J., Chang, K.-W., & Peng, N. (2026), arXiv:2610.26827
[link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.26827) · verified via arXiv API (published 2026-10-19)

Bears on: the seven-component decomposition of L(t), which has no term for condensation.

> One channel cuts across that list rather than sitting in it. Every pass that condenses the archive to fit storage or downlink is itself a loss event. KEEP-Bench measures how much of the evidence needed to support a conclusion survives context compression and finds that the best open-weight model preserves all required evidence in only 66.8% of cases and the best proprietary model in 61.6%, on inputs far shorter than the available context; evidence preserved per unit of inference compute peaks at moderate model scale, where recall remains low (Man et al., 2026). The measurements are on language models rather than on a mission archive, so the percentages do not transfer. The structural point does: condensation moves mass from G(t) into L(t), and a power-limited probe that summarizes to save storage pays in a term the integrity machinery cannot detect, because the surviving record still verifies.

Confidence: high. If accepted, L(t) arguably wants an eighth named component rather than a cross-cutting note.
