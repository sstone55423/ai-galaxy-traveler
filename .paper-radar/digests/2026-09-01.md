## Flagged (no insertion proposed)

- **Safety Does Not Compose: Non-Decaying Loop State for Autonomous LLM Agents**
  — Wu, C., Jia, H., Liu, Y., et al. (2026), arXiv:2608.27141
  [link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.27141) · verified via arXiv API
  (title, authors and 2026-08-27 date confirmed; the record has 11 authors, not
  the 8 the search returned).
  Formal separation result: against evidence fragmented across iterations, a
  monitor whose safety state resets at each trajectory boundary has true-positive
  rate equal to its false-positive rate however expressive it is, and a
  geometrically decaying risk score does not repair this because the adversary's
  required cooling-off period is a constant independent of the horizon N. The
  structural condition — an acceptance rule reproduced fresh with no accumulated
  cross-generation state — is exactly how the governed-amendment paper describes
  the immutable core, and the security paper already reasons about an adversary
  who knows that rule by construction. Not staged: the theorem concerns one
  agent's tool-call loop over hours against an active adversary, while the
  amendment problem is legitimacy and drift over 10^3–10^5 yr with no shared
  formalism. It would be an analogy dressed as a theorem. The security paper is
  the more honest home if it enters the series at all.

- **Can LLM Agents Discover? Evaluating Creativity on ML Engineering Tasks**
  — Bhushan, S., Zhang, Y., Wang, L. (2026), arXiv:2608.30047
  [link](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.30047) · verified via arXiv API.
  Measures declining P-Creativity (novelty relative to an agent's own prior
  solutions) as multi-turn research agents shift from exploration to
  exploitation. Closest thing this week to evidence on the knowledge-growth
  paper's claim that cruise is an active K-growth phase for a closed cognitive
  system with no new external input. Not staged: ten Kaggle-style tasks in a
  single run is not a millennia-long system, P-Creativity is not K, and the
  companion finding — LLMs show higher H-Creativity than medal-winning humans
  while performing worse — cuts against any simple reading.

- **Toward modeling principles for space energy system optimization**
  — Zwickl-Bernhard, S. (2026), *Advances in Applied Energy*
  [doi](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.adapen.2026.100302) · verified via Crossref
  (Crossref issues it 2026-09; the search record's 2026-08-28 is the online-first
  date).
  A methods-transfer review arguing space energy-system models need explicit
  material and commodity flows, thermodynamic constraints, mission-critical
  demand structures and uncertainty-aware optimization, with a lunar settlement
  case study. Same budgeting posture as the analytical engineering paper's
  four-budget model. Not staged: it offers preliminary modeling principles, not
  a closure metric or an order-of-magnitude result, so it neither supports nor
  challenges the finding that energy margins (~10^3) never bind while capability
  does.

- **Structural generalization and continual learning enabled by factorized
  entorhinal-hippocampal memory and entorhinal-parietal action circuits**
  — Hwang, J., Neupane, S., Jazayeri, M., Fiete, I. (2026), *bioRxiv*
  [doi](https://doi.org/10.64898/2026.08.25.747129) · verified via OpenAlex.
  A content-independent metric scaffold based on grid cells, coupled to a policy
  recurrent network, generalizes structurally to new environments where plain
  recurrent networks fail at all three tested generalizations. Maps onto the
  payload paper's separation of a stable structural substrate from mutable
  content. Not staged: a neuroscience modelling result on primate navigation
  tasks, addressing no specific claim about multi-millennium operation or value
  stability under self-modification. Worth having if the layered-memory section
  is ever expanded.
