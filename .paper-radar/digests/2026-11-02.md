### Governance paper -- Section 5, Biological Capability and Directed Panspermia

**Blown Sand on Mars Can Spread Microbial Spores from Earth** -- Fenton, L. K.,
Schuerger, A. C., Marshall, J. R., Kelley, K. L., & Smith, J. C. (2026), *Astrobiology*
[doi](https://doi.org/10.1177/15311074261504107) · verified via Crossref

Bears on: the claim that "capability yes, release no" is enforceable, i.e. that a probe
holding directed panspermia disabled in the immutable core has thereby bounded its
biological effect on an E1 to E3 world. The section states the bright line as a decision;
this result shows a decision isn't sufficient.

> Enforcement has a mechanical dimension that the bright line doesn't by itself cover.
> Release is a policy about intent, and intent doesn't govern mechanical transport. Fenton
> et al. (2026) accelerated sand grains to 5 m/s into a spore-laden aluminum surface and,
> of 187 identified B. subtilis endospores, found 74 (40%) still intact on the rebounding
> grains, concluding that natural sand transport could contaminate the environment in the
> vicinity of a landed spacecraft. On any world with mobile regolith and a working
> atmosphere, a probe can't treat its own footprint as the edge of its biological
> influence. The prohibition therefore has to be enforced through carried bioburden and
> limits on surface disturbance, not through a decision not to seed, which places part of
> its enforcement alongside the extraction restrictions of Section 6 rather than in the
> core prohibition alone.

Reference added: Fenton, L. K., Schuerger, A. C., Marshall, J. R., Kelley, K. L., &
Smith, J. C. (2026). Blown sand on Mars can spread microbial spores from Earth.
*Astrobiology*. https://doi.org/10.1177/15311074261504107

Confidence: high. Every figure in the prose is in the abstract, and the contamination
conclusion is the authors' own. Pairs with the existing COSPAR 2021 and OST Art. IX
citations: this is the mechanism those policies are trying to bound.

### Payload paper -- Section 3, A Mind That Improves Without Drifting

**Effective training pressure gates recursive knowledge degradation in LLMs: A multi-axis
dose-response study** -- Azancort Neto, J. L., de Mattos Teixeira, C. A., Ponce de Leon
Ferreira de Carvalho, A. C., & Francês, C. R. L. (2026), *Knowledge-Based Systems*
[doi](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.knosys.2026.117230) · verified via Crossref

Bears on: the architecture's claim that the mutable cognitive layer is "open to almost
unlimited self-modification". This puts a number on the "almost", and the dissociation it
reports attacks the core-integrity check directly: a check that samples stated values and
finds them intact can pass a lineage that is already degrading.

> The bound on "almost unlimited" is quantitative, and it sits in the per-generation
> modification budget rather than in the choice of adaptation method. Azancort Neto et al.
> (2026) ran recursive fine-tuning on synthetic data across three architectures and up to
> ten generations and mapped the outcome onto three regimes: homeostatic, bounded, and
> degradative. The transition between them is threshold-like in perturbation magnitude,
> with the boundary falling between effective ranks 50 and 88 for one architecture and
> between 3 and 6 for another, a tenfold difference; halving synthetic exposure moved one
> system toward the bounded regime, worth 9.2 percentage points of factual retention by the
> tenth generation. The result that matters most here is a dissociation they report at
> intermediate capacity: factual retention held near 90% while output-distribution quality
> collapsed threefold. A core-integrity check that samples stated values and finds them
> intact can therefore pass a lineage that is already degrading, which is why verification
> has to test behavior under load rather than recitation.

Reference added: Azancort Neto, J. L., de Mattos Teixeira, C. A., Ponce de Leon Ferreira de
Carvalho, A. C., & Francês, C. R. L. (2026). Effective training pressure gates recursive
knowledge degradation in LLMs: A multi-axis dose-response study. *Knowledge-Based Systems*.
https://doi.org/10.1016/j.knosys.2026.117230

Confidence: high on the figures, which are all in the abstract. The transfer from LLM
fine-tuning generations to a probe's cognitive layer is an analogy, and the prose is written
so the paper takes the regime structure and the dissociation, not the architectures. Their
Gemma 4 arm is single-seed and exploratory by their own statement; nothing here leans on it.
