Dispatch Probability

P(at least one probe succeeds) = 1 − (1 − p_leg)^n as a function of number of probes and hop distance. Illustrates the three-dispatch rule from the routing paper.

The single-probe ceiling

A single probe can never exceed p_stage^4 probability regardless of distance. At p_stage=0.9 that ceiling is 65.6%. Two probes cap at 88.2%. Three dispatches reliably clear 90% for targets within ~20 ly.
