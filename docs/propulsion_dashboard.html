The probe design in this series uses nuclear-electric at ~450 km/s (0.0015c). It's slow, but it can brake, settle, and run for millennia — the other two cannot.

The probe in this series sits far off this chart

The laser sail and fusion rocket are flyby concepts — they arrive in seconds or minutes at the target and cannot stop. The self-replicating seed in this series uses nuclear-electric at ~450 km/s (0.0015c), brakes against the interstellar medium using a magnetic sail, and settles. At that speed it takes ~2,800 years to reach Proxima Centauri. The trade is everything the other two concepts sacrifice: the ability to stop, build, and send children forward. See the vehicle paper for the full design rationale.
