Restrictiveness is not monotone in the index. The tightest constraints sit at the living-biosphere rungs E2–E3 and the non-communicative rung E5; cautious exchange becomes permissible at E6. E7 is a disposition, not an environment — it attaches to any party E2–E6. Its containment is unconditional (quarantine, isolate, reject); whether the node then engages is defaulted to no, with the security paper arguing the other way.
