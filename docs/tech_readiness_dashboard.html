Key Takeaways

Relatively small gaps

Launch speed (2.3x) and electronics lifetime (800x) are engineering problems on a 50–100 year horizon. No conceptual barriers remain; investment and time close them.

Large gaps — sustained programmes needed

Reactor longevity, archive storage, magnetic sail, and chip fabrication closure each span 3–7 orders of magnitude. They need dedicated, decade-scale research programmes.

No measurement baseline

AI cognitive stability and HTS tape longevity have no current measurement baseline. Closing these gaps requires conceptual breakthroughs, not just investment or time.
