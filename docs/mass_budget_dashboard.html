Key Insight

The manufacturing and ISRU plant dominates because it IS the mission — without it the probe cannot replicate or build. Computation costs essentially nothing by comparison at ~5%. Material and energy margins are vast (~10^18 and ~10^3 respectively); capability and manufacturing closure are the binding constraints.
