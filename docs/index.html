What this project is

Deep Time AI is not a fast starship proposal. It is a research platform for a slow, self-replicating interstellar probe architecture that can survive centuries, build local infrastructure, and expand a lineage without relying on an Earth return.

Slow, patient travel — the vehicle stops, becomes a settlement, and launches its children from local infrastructure.
Knowledge-centered mission — the aim is to carry and grow operational knowledge across generations.
Interactive evidence — the site includes real nearby star data plus Python-backed simulators for routing, power, and fleet growth.
