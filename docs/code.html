Multi-probe dispatch probability

P(at least one probe succeeds) = 1-(1-p)^n, computed live with sliders for hop distance and stage reliability. Shows the three-dispatch rule and why a single probe can never exceed 65.6% success regardless of distance.

Fuel scaling and radiator sizing

Two insights: supplementary fuel for longer hops costs well under 1% of seed mass per 10 ly — range is cheap. And radiator area drops from 14 m² at full manufacturing power to 0.5 m² at cruise.
