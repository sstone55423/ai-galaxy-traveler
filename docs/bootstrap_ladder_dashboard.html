The Closure Ladder — From Seed to Factory

The bootstrapping paper's L1–L5 capability hierarchy, and the vitamin arithmetic: what a settlement still cannot make, its children must carry. Companion to “From Seed to Factory” · deep-time-ai.org
Mature reference point: closure ~0.97 → a ~3% vitamin set, ~30 kg per tonne of child — the payload of irreducible parts (microelectronics, precision metrology, fissile inventory) carried down the lineage. Material and energy margins (~10¹⁸ and ~10³) never bind; capability does. L1–L3 extrapolate demonstrated capability; L4 is harder but conceivable; L5 is the research frontier.
