The model, honestly labeled: D(t) ≈ 2δ·N(t) — pairwise divergence carries the factor of two, D is a count of differently-resolved judgment calls (hence linear in decisions, not √t), and all four parameters are asserted, not derived. Front-loading follows from the paper's own mechanism: a newly isolated branch faces its densest interval of unprecedented decisions, so T_decision lengthens as the interpretive layer accumulates precedent and D(t) is concave. The shaded band is the lineage-network paper's reconnection window — the mesh begins forming at ~10⁴ yr and matures over ~10⁵. Divergence is front-loaded; reconnection is back-loaded. D_critical is defined against an observable (reconciliation-failure fraction at ledger audit); its value is the open empirical question.
