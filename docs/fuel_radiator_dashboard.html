Fuel & Radiators

Two findings from the subsystem budget: supplementary fuel for longer hops costs under 1% of seed mass per 10 ly — range is cheap. Radiator area needed during cruise is tiny compared to manufacturing mode, yet the same hardware serves both.

Range is cheap

At 150 W cruise and a 36 ly hop, the probe needs only 49.5 kg of U-235 — 1.34% of seed mass. Doubling the hop distance doubles the fuel, but both numbers are small. The constraint on range is not fuel mass; it is reliability over time and the probability of a viable host at destination.

Two regimes, one carried hardware

The 11 m² carried radiator is sized for full-power pulse events at settlement, not cruise. At 150 W cruise, only 0.52 m² is needed. At settlement manufacturing scale (14 kW thermal), hundreds to thousands of m² are built in situ from local material. Two regimes, one carried hardware.
